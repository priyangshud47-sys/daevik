# Daevik.in — Product Overview

**Stack:** Next.js (Frontend) · Node.js (Backend) · Supabase (Database) · NextAuth (Admin Authentication)

Daevik.in is a digital product sales website built for a single seller (not a multi-vendor marketplace) who sells multiple independent digital products, each with its own custom-designed landing page and checkout page. Unlike typical e-commerce platforms or SaaS builders, Daevik.in does **not** include an in-app drag-and-drop page builder. Instead, landing pages and checkout pages are designed externally (using AI tools like Antigravity) as static HTML/design assets and then uploaded/published into the platform per product.

The platform's core value is **operational simplicity with maximum design freedom**. Each product can look completely different — different layout, different branding, different funnel structure — since there is no shared template constraint. What ties everything together is a unified backend: a single home page product catalog, a unified checkout-to-payment-to-delivery pipeline, unified analytics, and a single admin panel to manage everything.

## Key Business Goals
- Launch new digital products quickly without redesigning the whole site
- Support multiple payment gateways flexibly, chosen per product
- Automate product delivery via email immediately after purchase
- Track marketing performance accurately via Facebook Conversions API
- Give the owner (admin) a clear, real-time view of sales, funnel drop-off, and customers — without page-building complexity

## Who Uses This
- **Customers:** Browse products on the home page → click a product → view its custom landing page → checkout → receive product via email.
- **Admin (site owner):** Uploads new products, assigns payment gateways, edits email templates, monitors sales/funnel data, manages SEO, and views customer records — all from one admin panel.

## What This Product Is NOT
- Not a multi-vendor marketplace
- Not a page builder / website builder
- Not a subscription/SaaS billing platform (one-time digital product purchases only, unless expanded later)
